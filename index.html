SOL_01 / MERCURY · BUDH

kaho

just kaho, it's written.

A voice dictation app for Mac. Press a key, speak, press the key again, and your words are typed wherever your cursor is: chat apps, email, documents, notes, browsers. It understands Hindi, English and Hinglish, both mixed in one sentence. Speech recognition and text cleanup run entirely on your Mac. Your audio and text are never uploaded.

Get Kaho

Lifetime licence for up to 5 of your own Macs. No subscription, no free trial. Pay with UPI, cards or bank options through Dodo Payments.

HOW IT WORKS

  1. 01

    Press Right Option once. A small listening pill appears at the bottom of the screen.

  2. 02

    Speak naturally.

  3. 03

    Press Right Option again, or click the tick on the pill. Kaho writes it out and types it where your cursor is.

If no text field is selected, Kaho offers to copy the text instead.

MODES

Raw

Exactly as heard.

Clean

Same words, tidied: fillers and false starts removed, punctuation fixed.

English

Writes everything in English.

Hinglish

Hindi and English mixed, in Latin letters.

Translate

Into another language.

Polish

Clearer, better organised writing.

ALSO IN KAHO

  • Dictionary. Add names and terms, like "Ujjawal" or "After Effects", and Kaho spells them right.
  • Edit by voice. Select text, press the hotkey and say what to change: "make this formal", "shorten this".
  • Tone that fits the app. More casual in chat apps, more formal in email.
  • History. Past dictations with one-click copy and retry. You choose how long history is kept, including never.
  • Stats. Words dictated, time saved and speaking speed, on the Home screen.
  • Your setup. Light and dark themes, optional launch at login, your choice of hotkey and microphone.

PRIVACY

  • Runs on your Mac. No account, no cloud, no subscription.
  • Internet is used only twice: once to download the speech models during setup (about 4.6 GB), and once to check your product key. After that Kaho works offline.

REQUIREMENTS

  • A Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer).
  • About 6 GB of free disk space.
  • Kaho does not run on Intel Macs, Windows or phones.

BUY AND INSTALL

  1. 01

    Click Get Kaho and pay on the Dodo Payments checkout.

  2. 02

    You get an email with your product key and a download link.

  3. 03

    Download the zip, double-click to unzip, drag Kaho into Applications and open it.

  4. 04

    The first time, macOS says it cannot verify Kaho, because Kaho is sold independently and not through the App Store. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. If Kaho does not appear there, open it once more.

  5. 05

    Paste your product key, let Kaho download its models, then allow Microphone and Accessibility when asked. Accessibility lets Kaho notice the hotkey and type the text where your cursor is.

FAQ

Does it work offline?

Yes, after the one-time setup.

Is my voice uploaded?

No. Everything runs on your Mac.

Which languages?

Hindi, English and Hinglish are the focus. Translate mode writes in other languages.

Why does macOS warn me when I open it?

Kaho is sold independently, not through the App Store, so the first time you open it macOS says it cannot verify it. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. If Kaho does not appear there, open it once more.

Can I use it on more than one Mac?

Yes, on up to 5 of your own Macs.

Windows or iPhone?

Not yet. Kaho is Mac only.

TOOL / PARAKHNA.COM

parakhna

The right device for your degree. One answer.

A device finder for students in India. Answer 7 quick questions about your course, budget and habits, and Parakhna matches you with the one laptop, desktop or tablet that fits, and tells you exactly why.

Free no sign up

7 questions · 60 seconds · one answer, plus two alternates.

Find my device

Prices shown are indicative street prices. Purchases through Parakhna's links may earn a small commission at no extra cost; recommendations are never influenced by it.

WHY IT EXISTS

Buying a study device in India is harder than the entrance exam.

Endless spec lists

400 laptops, 60 filters, and still no idea if 8GB is enough for B.Tech.

Paid rankings

"Top 10" articles that rank whoever pays the highest commission that month.

Spec jargon

H vs U processors, TGP, PPI. Nobody explains what actually matters for your course.

Overspending

Gaming rigs upsold to commerce students, underpowered machines sold to design students.

HOW IT WORKS

  1. 01

    Tell it about your student life. Your course, what you will actually do on the device, your budget, and whether it lives in a backpack or on a desk.

  2. 02

    It matches against a curated shortlist. Not 400 devices: around 45 genuinely good ones, hand-scored for coding, design, note-taking, gaming, battery and value.

  3. 03

    You get one pick, explained in plain words. Your best match plus two alternates, with the exact reasons each one fits. Then buy it wherever you like.

No sign up. No spam. No sponsored answers. Parakhna never asks for your phone number, and no brand can pay to become your recommendation.
