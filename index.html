TOOL / PARAKHNA.COM

parakhna

The right device for your degree. One answer.

A device finder for students in India. Answer 7 quick questions about your course, budget and habits, and Parakhna matches you with the one laptop, desktop or tablet that fits, and tells you exactly why.

Free no sign up

7 questions · 60 seconds · one answer, plus two alternates.

Find my device

Prices shown are indicative street prices. Purchases through Parakhna's links may earn a small commission at no extra cost; recommendations are never influenced by it.

WHY IT EXISTS

Buying a study device in India is harder than the entrance exam.

Endless spec lists

400 laptops, 60 filters, and still no idea if 8GB is enough for B.Tech.

Paid rankings

"Top 10" articles that rank whoever pays the highest commission that month.

Spec jargon

H vs U processors, TGP, PPI. Nobody explains what actually matters for your course.

Overspending

Gaming rigs upsold to commerce students, underpowered machines sold to design students.

HOW IT WORKS

  1. 01

    Tell it about your student life. Your course, what you will actually do on the device, your budget, and whether it lives in a backpack or on a desk.

  2. 02

    It matches against a curated shortlist. Not 400 devices: around 45 genuinely good ones, hand-scored for coding, design, note-taking, gaming, battery and value.

  3. 03

    You get one pick, explained in plain words. Your best match plus two alternates, with the exact reasons each one fits. Then buy it wherever you like.

No sign up. No spam. No sponsored answers. Parakhna never asks for your phone number, and no brand can pay to become your recommendation.
